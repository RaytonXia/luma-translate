# Third-party notices

The bundled English–Chinese dictionary is derived from ECDICT at commit
`bc015ed2e24a7abef49fc6dbbb7fe32c1dadaf8b`, with 767,778 eligible records,
34 original academic/phrase supplements and the existing Singapore overlay.
The upstream MIT license is included in `Sources/LumaTranslate/Resources/ECDICT_LICENSE.txt`
and in the installed app's resources. Provenance, coverage and hashes are in
`Sources/LumaTranslate/Resources/dictionary-manifest.json`.

Supplement examples are original language examples, not research findings or
quotations. The upstream repository aggregates several earlier dictionaries and
word lists; its provenance should be reviewed for the intended distribution.

Apple Vision OCR and AVSpeechSynthesizer use macOS system components. Optional
DeepSeek and Gemini requests require the user's own key and permission to send
recognized English. No API credentials, third-party audio services or models are bundled.
